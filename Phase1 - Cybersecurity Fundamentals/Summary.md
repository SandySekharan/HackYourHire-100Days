# 🟢 PHASE 1 — Cybersecurity Fundamentals

## Days 1–10

Don't go too deep. You need to be able to **explain the concepts clearly**.

### Day 1 — CIA Triad

Learn:

* Confidentiality
* Integrity
* Availability
* Authentication vs Authorization
* Non-repudiation

**Interview question:**

> What is the CIA triad? Give a real-world example.

---

### Day 2 — Security Controls

Learn:

* Preventive
* Detective
* Corrective
* Compensating controls
* Technical / Administrative / Physical controls

Practice identifying controls from real systems.

---

### Day 3 — Risk

Understand:

**Asset → Threat → Vulnerability → Risk → Control**

Learn:

* Likelihood
* Impact
* Risk assessment
* Risk treatment

Interview:

> What is the difference between vulnerability and risk?

---

### Day 4 — Vulnerabilities

Learn:

* CVE
* CWE
* CVSS
* Exploit
* Zero-day
* Vulnerability vs exposure

Understand what CVSS actually means rather than memorizing scores.

---

### Day 5 — Authentication

Learn:

* Password authentication
* MFA
* Certificates
* Tokens
* Session
* OAuth
* OpenID Connect
* SAML — basic understanding

Interview:

> Authentication vs authorization?

---

### Day 6 — Cryptography

Learn only the fundamentals:

* Encryption
* Hashing
* Encoding
* Symmetric encryption
* Asymmetric encryption
* Digital signatures
* Certificates
* TLS

Be able to explain:

> What happens when you open HTTPS website?

---

### Day 7 — IAM

Learn:

* Least privilege
* RBAC
* ABAC
* Service accounts
* Privileged accounts
* Secrets

---

### Day 8 — Logging & Monitoring

Understand:

* Logs
* SIEM
* SOC
* Alert
* Incident
* IOC
* Detection

---

### Day 9 — Incident Response

Learn:

**Preparation → Identification → Containment → Eradication → Recovery → Lessons Learned**

Interview:

> A server is compromised. What will you do?

---

### Day 10 — Revision + Mini Interview

Without looking at notes, explain:

1. CIA
2. Risk
3. CVE/CWE/CVSS
4. Authentication
5. Encryption
6. IAM
7. Incident response

**Goal:** explain each in **1–2 minutes**.

---
